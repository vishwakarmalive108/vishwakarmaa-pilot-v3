RISK CONTROL
CORE RULE

Temporary work is free — unless an active permanent record exists elsewhere.

If a worker has a permanent record at another registered mill, the gate creates a protected red flag and requires NOC. An approved NOC can permit the worker under association rules.

V53 PRINCIPLE

We are enforcing the actual association rule—not turning every daily labourer into a restricted worker. The restriction attaches only to an active permanent employment record and is resolved through NOC/association governance.
