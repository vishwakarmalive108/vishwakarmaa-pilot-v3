🛡️ Vishwakarmaa V1 Final

Missing gaps solved — implementation-ready blueprint
🪪 Identity & ConsentLOCKED
VKID unique रहेगा। Identity verification level, consent, verification date और permitted data-sharing scope record होंगे। Government ID का raw number unnecessary screens पर नहीं दिखेगा।
🚩 Red Flag + RecoveryLOCKED
Advance recovery, unauthorized work, attendance/financial dispute और other concerns अलग case types होंगे। Evidence → review → decision → recovery/closure पूरा lifecycle रहेगा। Public blacklist नहीं।
🌐 Network ActivityLOCKED
Relevant events: registration, verified gate entry, attendance, employment link और NOC events। दूसरे mill का private ledger नहीं। Visibility role + Association rules से तय होगी।
📴 Offline + SyncLOCKED
Offline entries encrypted local queue में जाएँगी। Online होने पर server validation + sync होगा। Conflict होने पर silent overwrite नहीं; audit + authorized resolution.
✏️ Correction RulesLOCKED
Same-day operational correction limited role को। पुराने records के लिए Owner approval. हर change में before/after, user, time और reason.
👥 Permission MatrixLOCKED
Owner, Partner, Manager, Supervisor, Gate, Accounts और custom roles. UI permission के साथ server-side authorization भी mandatory.
🏭 Multiple MillsREADY
एक Owner के My Mills में कई mills. हर mill का attendance, ledger, staff और private business data अलग scope में रहेगा.
👥 Hamal TeamsREADY
6–15 member team, leader, team attendance, work quantity, rate और settlement. Individual members की history भी बनी रहेगी.
🔔 Smart AlertsREADY
Immediate: Red Flag / Owner order. Scheduled: Daily Report / NOC expiry. Routine attendance notifications नहीं.
📊 ReportsREADY
Daily, weekly, monthly, worker-wise, labour-cost, Hamal-cost, attendance और outstanding-advance reports.
🧾 Payment ProofREADY
Cash/Bank/UPI, reference number, optional proof, duplicate-reference validation और immutable payment history.
🚨 Incident ModuleREADY
Accident, fight, damage, theft या other incident अलग confidential case में record होगा; Red Flag से automatically mix नहीं होगा.
🧑‍🔧 Worker AccessV1 OPTIONAL
V1 में Worker Card + QR पर्याप्त. Full worker app बाद में, जहाँ worker अपनी attendance और settlement देख सके.
Final rule: Privacy by default · minimum necessary data · explicit permissions · server-side authorization · audit trail · no silent deletion · no automatic guilt/blacklisting.
