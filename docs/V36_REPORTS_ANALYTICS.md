# V36 Reports & Analytics

## Factory reports
- Attendance rate and trend
- Workforce mix
- Daily/Fatak labour activity
- Cross-mill blocks
- NOC activity
- Red Flag summary
- Payroll gross/net/overtime/deductions
- Audit reporting

## Data model
Reports are generated from source-of-truth operational tables rather than duplicated manual records.

## Privacy
Employee-level sensitive data remains role-controlled. Aggregate reports can be shared at association level without exposing unnecessary personal information.

## Production extensions
- CSV/PDF export
- Scheduled monthly reports
- Shift/department filters
- Date-range comparison
- Payroll statutory reporting
- Association aggregate dashboard
